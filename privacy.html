Tayibat Life Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

English

Tayibat Life provides general nutrition and wellness information. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

The app stores settings, favorites, water entries, weight entries, premium status, and simple usage events locally on your device using browser or app storage. Tayibat Life does not require an account and does not upload this local data to our servers.

If you contact support or send feedback by email, your email app sends the message to the listed support address and the email provider may process that message under its own privacy terms.

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics according to Google's advertising policies and Google Play requirements. You can reset or limit your Android advertising identifier in device settings.

The release configuration disables external PayPal payments. Any future paid digital features distributed through Google Play should use Google Play Billing unless an official policy exception applies.
